# Licensing

Every source and layer retains its licence evidence, attribution, access date, and redistribution condition. Public delivery is authorised by a separate `licenceDecision` with an explicit state, use scope, review date, and exact evidence snapshot. The decision is invalidated when the retained licence text changes. Free text is never parsed as approval. Do not embed or export third-party map imagery unless the recorded decision explicitly allows the relevant public use.

Missing, stale, unresolved, denied, restricted, or internal-only decisions are publication blockers. An `inherited` decision is valid only when it delegates to source records and every retained source record has a matching public-use approval. Static catalogue decisions are recorded in `data/licensing/public-licence-evidence.json`; unregistered text fails closed. Database and newly imported records receive no implicit approval.

Public packages containing an OpenStreetMap-derived component add computed component metadata naming the ODbL 1.0 and `© OpenStreetMap contributors`. Map credits are additive: a configured tile-provider credit cannot replace the OSM credit. This records provenance and the OSM component's licence status; it does not encode a legal conclusion about whether the overall Townscape delivery is a derivative database or collective database. That classification remains for legal review before commercial release.
