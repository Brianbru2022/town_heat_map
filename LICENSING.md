# Licensing

Every source and layer records its licence, attribution, access date, and redistribution condition. Do not embed or export third-party map imagery unless the relevant terms explicitly allow it. The application keeps restricted map services as URLs and disables raster-inclusive exports when terms or CORS permission are unclear.

Missing or explicitly unresolved redistribution terms are publication blockers, including free-text terms that say reuse still requires review. Citation-only material may remain publishable when Townscape Guides redistributes no source text or media; the restriction remains an advisory that must be honoured. A licence declaration that delegates to individual source records is a blocker if any relevant source licence is absent or unresolved.

Public packages containing an OpenStreetMap-derived component add computed component metadata naming the ODbL 1.0 and `© OpenStreetMap contributors`. Map credits are additive: a configured tile-provider credit cannot replace the OSM credit. This records provenance and the OSM component's licence status; it does not encode a legal conclusion about whether the overall Townscape delivery is a derivative database or collective database. That classification remains for legal review before commercial release.
